Not on your site yet

6 questions your site can't answer today.

These answers live in your engagement letters, tax organizers and staff inboxes, not on the site.

  • What does a business tax return or monthly bookkeeping cost?Owners compare CPA firms on price before they fill in a form, and the site gives no fee or fee basis.
  • Is there a minimum fee to take us on?A small business or an individual wants to know they qualify before reaching out, and the site doesn't say.
  • How does switching to you from our current CPA work?Prior-year files, mid-year timing and handoff are the real worries of a switch, and no page walks through them.
  • What documents should we gather for our first year with you?Without a checklist, the first meetings go to chasing paperwork instead of planning.
  • Which partner would lead our engagement?The site promises the same team year after year, but not how a new client is matched to one.
  • When do you need our paperwork to file on time?Every client asks this before tax season, and while the Key Dates post lists IRS deadlines, no page says when you need their documents or when you'd file an extension instead.
