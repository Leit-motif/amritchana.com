Is there a cheaper inspection option if our façade is in good shape?+

Yes. Chicago's Short Form Only program lets qualifying, well-maintained buildings file Ongoing Reports instead of full Critical Exams, which reduces inspection costs.

“This streamlined process benefits buildings with well-maintained facades, reducing inspection costs while maintaining compliance.”From Façade Ordinance Inspections page
After the inspection, can you help us scope and bid out the repairs?+

Yes. Inspection findings can feed repair scopes, construction documents and competitive bids for the repair work.

“Our findings can also be leveraged to develop repair scopes, create construction documents, and solicit competitive bids for repair work, supporting cost-effective and timely facade maintenance.”From Façade Ordinance Inspections page
Which Philadelphia buildings need façade inspections?+

Buildings six stories or taller, or with any appurtenance over 60 feet, need evaluations by a licensed professional.

“Buildings that are six stories or taller, or have any appurtenance exceeding 60 feet in height, are subject to mandatory evaluations by a licensed professional.”From Façade Ordinance Inspections page

Not on your site yet

6 questions your site can't answer today.

These answers live in K&H's proposals, past reports and inboxes, not on the site.

  • What does a Critical Examination or Short Form report cost for a building our size?Condo boards have to put a number in the budget before they can approve any inspection.
  • Our ordinance deadline is close. How fast can you inspect and file?Missed filings bring city fines, so boards hire whoever can commit to a date.
  • What does a reserve study cost, and how long until we have the report?Associations usually need the study before a budget meeting or an owners' vote.
  • What have façade repairs cost on buildings like ours?Owners want a realistic range before an inspection turns up work they must fund.
  • Can we see a sample report before we sign?Boards compare deliverables between firms, and the site shows none.
  • How soon will someone get back to us after we submit the form?The contact page says only that the team will be in touch 'shortly', which is hard to plan around.
