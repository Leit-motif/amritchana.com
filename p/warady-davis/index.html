Not on your site yet

6 questions your site can't answer today.

These answers live in your engagement letters, proposals and partners' inboxes, not on the site.

  • How do you price an audit, review or tax engagement: fixed fee or hourly?Owners who are switching firms compare fees first, and the site calls its fees cost-effective without saying how an engagement is priced.
  • How do we move over from our current CPA mid-year, and what does onboarding involve?Switching cost is the main reason owners stay put, and the site doesn't explain the transition.
  • Who would be my day-to-day contact, and how often would a partner be involved?Partner access is why owners choose a mid-size firm, and the site doesn't say how a client is staffed.
  • How fast can you turn around reviewed or audited statements before a bonding or bank deadline?Contractors live on surety and bank deadlines, and the site promises bonding help without any turnaround times.
  • What does a succession planning engagement cost, and how long does it take?An owner considering an exit wants to know the scope and timeline before booking a meeting.
  • Is my company too small, or too large, for you?The site says 'all sizes' and cites valuations for companies with sales over $500 million, but gives no size range or minimum for audit and tax clients, so owners can't tell whether they fit.
