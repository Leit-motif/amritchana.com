Not on your site yet

6 questions your site can't answer today.

These answers live in CEMCON's proposals, past project files and inboxes, not on the site.

  • How do you price a site development or municipal job: hourly, lump sum or not-to-exceed?Developers budget engineering early, and with no fee guidance the first call is spent on basics instead of scope.
  • How long does a typical site plan take from kickoff to final engineering approval?Schedule drives a developer's land closing and financing dates, so it is often the first question asked.
  • Who would actually lead my project, and what is their background?The site introduces no project managers or their experience, so buyers cannot judge who they would be working with.
  • Can I see comparable projects with scope, size and the client named?Project photos carry only short captions, so a municipality or developer cannot match their job to past work or ask for a reference.
  • Which communities do you currently serve, and do you act as a village engineer anywhere?Municipal buyers want proof of current, ongoing relationships with towns like theirs.
  • Are you IDOT-prequalified, and in which categories?Public transportation work often requires prequalification, and an agency screening consultants checks it first.
