Not on your site yet

6 questions your site can't answer today.

These answers live in your quotes, submittals, credit forms and project managers' inboxes, not on the site.

  • What's the cutoff for same-day delivery, and what does it cost?The site lists same-day, weekend and second runs but no cutoffs or fees, and a foreman deciding whether to send a truck needs both.
  • How fast can you turn around a lighting package quote?Contractors bidding a job need the lighting number before bid day, and the design pages give no turnaround.
  • What are current lead times on switchgear and panels?Gear lead times drive whole project schedules, and the gear page mentions quick-ship options but gives no lead times.
  • How long does approval take for a new credit account?The credit resources page explains escrow and job-account approvals, but a contractor opening a first account only gets a PDF application with no timeline.
  • What's your return and restocking policy?Overbought material is a cost on every job, and no web page states the policy.
  • Is it in stock at the counter right now?There's no online catalog, and the category headings send contractors to a different site, so stock checks become phone calls.
