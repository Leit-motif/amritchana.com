Not on your site yet

6 questions your site can't answer today.

These answers live in JHA's proposals, project files and inboxes, not on the site.

  • What does an ALTA survey cost, and how fast can you turn one around?Developers on a closing deadline ask this first, and without it they call whoever answers quickest.
  • How do you price site engineering: lump sum, hourly, or by phase?Owners need a budget number before they will schedule a scoping call.
  • How long does a Phase I environmental site assessment take?Lenders and buyers set due-diligence windows around this, so the timeline decides who gets the job.
  • What do you need from me to scope a new site and send a proposal?Developers want to send the right survey, site plan and title documents up front so the first call ends with a number.
  • What exactly did you do on a project like mine, and can I speak with that client?Many portfolio entries give only a client, a location and one line, and none offer a reference contact, so a buyer cannot compare scope or check with a past client.
  • Which villages have you taken projects through approvals with?Portfolio entries name the town but not the approvals involved, and developers want an engineer who already knows the local reviewer.
