Not on your site yet

6 questions your site can't answer today.

These answers live in your engagement letters, intake checklists and attorneys' inboxes, not on the site.

  • What does an estate plan cost: flat fee or hourly?Families compare firms on price before they book, and the private client pages say you can help at any net worth but give no fee basis.
  • Who would be my attorney, and how do you match me with one?With 69 attorneys, a new client wants to know who they'd actually work with, and no page explains how matters are assigned.
  • What should I bring to a first estate planning meeting?Without a checklist, the first meeting gets spent gathering documents instead of planning.
  • When will I hear back after emailing info@chuhak.com?The site's contact buttons all open an email to that one inbox, and no page says who reads it or how soon they reply.
  • Is the first consultation free?It's the question that decides whether a family or owner reaches out at all, and the pages don't say.
  • What happens step by step after someone dies and we call you?Executors want the process and the rough timeline before they commit, and the probate copy describes what you do, not what they should expect.

Making it real

Three weeks to first questions from new private clients answered the same day.

Families and business owners write to info@ with the same first questions about estate plans, probate and fees. We'd start there, because the answers already exist in your checklists, engagement letters and past replies.

Week 1

Pick one workflow

We'd start with new private-client intake: the first questions families and owners send before an estate planning or probate meeting, routed to the right attorney with a drafted answer. We measure how it runs today and agree 20 to 30 tests it has to pass.

Week 2

Build your company brain

Your documents, with sources and permissions, wired into that one workflow and the handoff to your people.

Week 3

Train and measure

Your team uses it for real. You get a before and after scorecard against the baseline.
