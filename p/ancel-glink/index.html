Answered from your site

12 questions local governments ask, answered with your own words.

Every answer quotes a page on ancelglink.com. If a quote couldn't be found on the site, the answer didn't make the cut.

What kinds of local governments do you represent?+

The firm's local government practice covers the full range of Illinois local bodies, from municipalities and townships to schools and counties.

“Ancel Glink’s local government practice offers comprehensive legal services to municipalities, park districts, townships, libraries, schools, counties, and other local governments.”From Local Government page
We already have a village attorney. Can you act as special counsel on one project?+

Yes. The firm says it serves as special counsel to municipalities on special projects or matters, such as prosecution, labor, litigation or land use.

“We also serve as special counsel to municipalities that need our assistance on special projects or matters, including serving as municipal prosecutor, special labor counsel, defending litigation matters, or providing specialized land use services.”From Local Government page
Will an attorney attend our board meetings?+

Attending board, council, commission and committee meetings is listed as regular work the firm does for its local government clients.

“Regularly attend board, council, commission, and committee meetings on behalf of our local government clients.”From Local Government page
How quickly do you respond?+

The firm says it aims to respond the same day and is reachable by email, text and phone.

“Ancel Glink understands that the business of local government moves quickly and requires being promptly responsive, we strive to respond within the same day; and we are always available by email, text and phone.”From Core Values page
Can you train our newly elected officials and staff?+

Training officials and employees on topics such as FOIA, open meetings, meeting procedure and ethics, including newly elected officials training, is part of the firm's local government work.

“Provide training to local government officials and employees on a variety of legal issues, including FOIA, open meetings, board meeting procedures, ethics, First Amendment audits, social media use, and newly elected officials training.”From Local Government page
Will you keep us informed when new laws pass?+

The firm says clients are briefed when new state or federal legislation passes, including on approaches to compliance.

“When new state or federal legislation is passed, our clients are fully briefed on the impact, as well as creative approaches for compliance.”From Local Government page
Do you represent park districts?+

Yes. The firm says it represents more than 50 park districts and related park and recreation bodies across Illinois.

“Ancel Glink represents more than 50 park districts, municipal park and recreation departments, forest preserve and conservation districts and special recreation associations across Illinois.”From Local Government page
Do you represent libraries?+

Yes. The firm says it represents more than 70 libraries and library districts as general counsel.

“Ancel Glink represents more than 70 libraries and library districts throughout the state, serving as the library’s general counsel.”From Local Government page
Do you work with school districts?+

Yes: as general attorney, in litigation and on special matters, including employment matters and collective bargaining negotiations.

“We represent school districts as their general attorney, in litigation, and as counsel on special matters, including advising districts on employment matters and assisting in collective bargaining negotiations.”From Local Government page
We're a developer, not a government. Do you work with private clients?+

Yes. The land use team says it advises property owners and developers as well as local governments.

“Our attorneys regularly advise local governments, property owners, and developers on economic development, tax increment financing, real estate, construction, environmental, green energy, and telecommunications matters.”From Land Use & Economic Development page
Do you handle zoning approvals for data centers or renewable energy?+

The land use page lists securing zoning approval for data centers and renewable energy facilities among its example services.

“Securing zoning approval for emerging technologies, including data centers and renewable energy facilities.”From Land Use & Economic Development page
Do you have an office near us?+

The firm has offices across Illinois, and says clients can visit a local office while drawing on all of them.

“Ancel Glink has law offices throughout the entire state of Illinois. Our clients can visit a local office, while having the resources of all our offices.”From Contact page

Not on your site yet

5 questions your site can't answer today.

These answers live in your engagement letters, RFQ responses and attorneys' inboxes, not on the site.

  • How is a general counsel engagement billed: retainer, hourly, or a mix?Boards budget legal spend a year ahead, and the pages call the work cost-effective but never say how it is priced.
  • Which attorney would be our main point of contact?A village choosing counsel wants a named person, and the practice pages describe the firm, not who would be assigned.
  • What happens when we switch to you from our current attorney?File transfer, conflict checks and the first board meeting are the real worries of a switch, and no page walks through them.
  • Which communities near us could we call for a reference?The park district section invites prospects to talk to your clients, but the site doesn't say which clients to call.
  • How do you handle conflicts when you represent neighboring communities?You draft boundary agreements between neighboring municipalities, so a prospect will ask how both sides are protected.

Train and measure
