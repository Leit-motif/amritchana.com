Answered from your site

12 questions public agencies ask, answered with your own words.

Every answer quotes a page on ciorba.com. If a quote couldn't be found on the site, the answer didn't make the cut.

Who are your main clients today?+

Local, county and state agencies, continuing a practice that goes back to 1927.

“Today, Ciorba Group continues the tradition started by Mr. Walther of providing quality engineering services to local, county, and state agencies.”From History page
Do you also work for developers? Could that conflict with our village's interests?+

Ciorba stepped away from land development in the early 1980s specifically to avoid conflicts with its municipal clients.

“In the early 1980s, Ciorba Group moved away from land development to avoid conflicts with its growing number of municipal clients.”From History page
Have you worked for IDOT or the Illinois Tollway?+

Yes. Ciorba has done transportation and structural work for IDOT and the Illinois State Toll Highway Authority since the 1970s.

“During the 1970s, the firm ventured into transportation and structural engineering for state agencies — such as the Illinois Department of Transportation and the Illinois State Toll Highway Authority.”From History page
What kinds of bridges and structures do you design?+

Everything from simple single-span bridges, culverts and retaining walls to complex-geometry structures that need advanced analysis.

“Our structural engineering team, in conjunction with our roadway and water resources groups, successfully designs structures ranging from simple, single span bridges, culverts and retaining walls to structures with complex geometry that require advanced structural analysis.”From Bridge Design & Structural Engineering page
Do you do bridge inspections and load ratings?+

Yes. Ciorba covers the full bridge process, from inspections and load ratings to concept studies and rehab or replacement plans.

“We master the total process of completing bridge improvements from inspections to load ratings of existing facilities, to concept studies and design plans for rehabilitation or new structures.”From Bridge Design & Structural Engineering page
Will the design be economical to build and maintain?+

That is the stated aim: structural solutions that are economical and easy to build and maintain, at any project size.

“For projects of any size and complexity, we develop distinctive structural solutions that are not only economical, but easy to build and maintain.”From Bridge Design & Structural Engineering page
Can you provide a resident engineer during construction?+

Yes. For agencies and private clients, Ciorba provides onsite Resident and Construction Engineers who check the contractor's work against the plans and specs.

“For government agencies and private clients, Ciorba’s services include providing onsite Resident and Construction Engineers to monitor the contractors work for conformance with the plans and specifications.”From Construction Engineering page
Do you work for contractors too?+

Yes. Contractors use Ciorba for temporary support systems, haul-road geometrics and pavement, and temporary drainage.

“Contractors often call on Ciorba to design support systems for new structure construction, geometrics and pavement for haul roads, or temporary drainage facilities.”From Construction Engineering page
Will your engineers understand what matters to our residents and businesses, not just the engineering?+

That is how Ciorba describes its municipal work: engineers who understand what officials, residents and businesses care about.

“An engineer must be able to look beyond the technical aspect of a project and have the skills to understand what is important to municipal officials, residents, and businesses.”From Municipal & Civil Engineering page
Can you help us manage the infrastructure we already have?+

Yes. The asset management team sets a baseline condition and builds a maintenance program to extend asset life.

“Our asset management team can develop a baseline condition of a client’s infrastructure and then assist in developing a maintenance program to extend its life and avoid the cost of its premature replacement.”From Municipal & Civil Engineering page
Can you help with localized flooding?+

Yes. Ciorba studies and designs new stormwater facilities, both for transportation projects and to relieve local flooding.

“Ciorba Group studies and designs new stormwater facilities for transportation improvements as well as to alleviate localized flooding problems.”From Water Resources Engineering page
We're a small village. Can you handle our water and sewer work?+

Ciorba says its water resources staff can meet the needs of a community of any size.

“When it comes to water and your environment, our staff can satisfy the needs of any size community.”From Water Resources Engineering page

Not on your site yet

6 questions your site can't answer today.

These answers live in Ciorba's statements of qualifications, proposals and inboxes, not on the site.

  • Which IDOT prequalification categories do you hold, and are you DBE-certified?Agencies screen consultants on prequalification before anything else, and the site doesn't list it.
  • Can we see a sample of the reports or plan review letters you send a village?Boards compare deliverables between firms before they change engineers.
  • How fast do you turn around a developer's plan review for our village?Slow reviews stall development, and staff hear about it from the board and from developers.
  • How long does Phase I and Phase II engineering usually take on a local bridge or road job?Funding deadlines and letting dates drive the agency's schedule.
  • How do you structure fees for ongoing village engineer services?Municipal budgets are set a year ahead, and retainer versus hourly changes the line item.
  • What did a comparable project cost, and how did it go?Boards and staff want a real precedent before they commit public money.
