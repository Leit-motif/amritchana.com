Not on your site yet

6 questions your site can't answer today.

These answers live in your proposals, fee letters and principals' inboxes, not on the site.

  • How do you structure your fee: percentage of construction, fixed fee, or hourly?A developer builds the pro forma around design fees, so having to wait for a proposal just to learn this slows the shortlist.
  • How long does a typical mid-rise multifamily take from schematic design to permit?Schedule drives carry costs and lender timing, and the site shows outcomes but no typical durations.
  • Who from your team would lead my project, and what else are they working on now?Principal bios list past projects, but nothing says how a principal is matched to a new pursuit or what each one is carrying right now.
  • How do you run a Chicago planned development or zoning approval, including community meetings?Approvals appear as headlines, but the process, the roles and the usual pitfalls aren't explained anywhere.
  • How do you keep design inside the construction budget during development?Owners want to know about cost check-ins, estimating partners and value engineering before they hire, and the site doesn't cover it.
  • Which past developer clients can I call as references for a project like mine?Repeat clients are mentioned but none are offered as references, so a buyer has to ask and wait.
