Not on your site yet

6 questions your site can't answer today.

These answers live in your branch procedures, credit files, vendor lead-time sheets and counter staff's heads, not on the site.

  • How long does credit approval take, and what are the terms?A contractor with a job starting needs to know when they can buy on account, and the application only says 'standard payment terms apply'.
  • What's the cutoff for same-day delivery to my jobsite?Crews lose hours waiting on parts, and the site only says to schedule delivery with your branch.
  • How do I reach after-hours service, and what does it cost?No-heat and no-cool calls don't wait for the counter to open, and the services page lists After Hours Service without a number, hours or charge.
  • How do I return a part or file a warranty claim?Warranty paperwork is a daily cost for service contractors, and the site has no returns or warranty page.
  • What's the lead time on rooftop units and VRF equipment?Lead time decides whether a contractor can promise a replacement date, and the site gives none.
  • What do you pay for recovered refrigerant, and how do I return cylinders?The services page lists refrigerant buy back and reclaim, but not the rates, accepted gases or the steps, so contractors have to call to find out what it's worth.
