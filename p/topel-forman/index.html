5 questions your site can't answer today.

These answers live in your engagement letters, tax organizers and partners' inboxes, not on the site.

  • How are your fees set for a tax return or an audit?Owners compare CPA firms on cost before they reach out, and the site gives no fee or fee basis.
  • Who would be assigned to me, and how is that decided?With partners across many industry groups, a new client wants to know who they'd work with, and no page explains how that's decided.
  • What documents should I gather before our first meeting?Without a checklist, the first meetings go to chasing paperwork instead of planning.
  • How does switching from our current CPA mid-year work?Prior-year files and handoff timing are the real worries of a switch, and no page walks through them.
  • When do you need our paperwork to file on time?Every client asks this before tax season, and while the blog covers IRS deadlines, no page says when you need their documents or when you'd file an extension instead.
