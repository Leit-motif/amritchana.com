Not on your site yet

6 questions your site can't answer today.

These answers live in your proposals, board presentations and project files, not on the site.

  • How is your fee structured when you're both architect and construction manager?Combined A/CM is the firm's standout model, and a board can't compare it with a separate CM without knowing how the fees work.
  • What does 'referendum assistance' include, and how have your clients' referendums turned out?It appears as a single line in the services list, but it's the first thing a district facing a vote needs to understand.
  • Which engineers are on your own staff and which are outside consultants?The site calls it a 'network of professionals', and owners want to know who carries the liability and the coordination.
  • How long does a new school typically take from board approval to opening day?Districts plan around one fixed opening date, and the site gives outcomes but not typical schedules.
  • Which of your offices would staff our project, and who would lead it?With offices in six states and Spain, a buyer can't tell which office or which principal would run their project.
  • What does your Guaranteed Maximum Price delivery actually guarantee, and who carries the risk if bids come in high?It's listed as a service with no explanation, and public owners need that answer before choosing a delivery method.
