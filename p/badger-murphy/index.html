6 questions your site can't answer today.

  • Which days do you deliver to my area, and what's the order cutoff?A kitchen plans prep around the truck, and the site says only 'weekday deliveries throughout the Chicagoland area'.
  • Is there a minimum order for delivery?Small restaurants and bakeries rule distributors in or out on the drop minimum, and no page names one.
  • How do I open an account, and what are the payment terms?A new buyer can't place a first order without an account, and the site offers no application, credit terms or steps.
  • Can I see prices and order online?The online catalog shows item number, brand, pack and size but no prices, so a buyer checking a price has to wait for the weekly price sheet, call, or request a quote that can take up to three business days.
  • Can you send allergen and spec sheets for an item?Bakeries and institutions need ingredient and allergen data before they switch products, and product pages carry none.
  • How do I get a credit for damaged or short product?Buyers want to know returns are painless before they move their business, and no page covers credits or returns.
