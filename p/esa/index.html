Answered from your site

12 questions owners ask, answered with your own words.

Every answer quotes a page on esadesign.com. If a quote couldn't be found on the site, the answer didn't make the cut.

How big is the firm?+

Founded in 1983, ESA now has nearly 50 architects and interior designers.

“Over the past 40 years, the Eckenhoff Saunders team has expanded to nearly 50 architects and interior design professionals.”From The Firm page
Do clients come back to you?+

Yes. ESA's first client still works with the firm.

“This approach has rewarded us with client relationships that are unusually prolific: ESA’s first client still works with the firm.”From The Firm page
How involved will we be in the design?+

Every phase, from inception through completion, is run as a collaboration between the client and the ESA team.

“From a project’s inception through its completion and beyond, every phase of the design process is a collaborative effort between our clients and the ESA team.”From The Firm page
Will we stay in close contact through construction?+

Yes. ESA says its size keeps close client contact at every phase, from pre-design through construction administration.

“The expertise of our design team allows us to offer a wide range of architectural services and specializations while our size ensures close client contact at every project phase, from pre-design through construction administration.”From The Firm page (Services)
How do you catch coordination problems before construction?+

They combine Revit and Navisworks during construction documents to find and fix cross-discipline conflicts early.

“We have found that combining the BIM programs Autodesk Revit Architecture and Navisworks during the construction documents phase helps us identify and correct potential problems across design and engineering disciplines early on, resulting in more coordinated documents and a smoother construction process.”From The Firm page (BIM Integration)
Have you designed behavioral health units?+

Yes. ESA is lead architect for Riverside Healthcare's renovated Adult Behavioral Health Inpatient Unit, designed for safety, dignity and healing.

“The new unit was thoughtfully designed to support safety, dignity and healing, with private patient rooms, calming environments and lighting that reflects natural daylight cycles.”From Riverside Healthcare article
Can you phase a multi-year hospital program?+

Yes. The Riverside program continues in phases through 2027, with pediatric and geriatric behavioral health renovations still to come.

“Additional phases of the project will continue through 2027 and include renovations to Riverside’s pediatric and geriatric behavioral health spaces.”From Riverside Healthcare article
Have you renovated a space you originally designed?+

Yes. Northwestern Memorial brought ESA back to renovate a clinical suite the firm had completed more than 15 years earlier.

“Northwestern Memorial Hospital engaged Eckenhoff Saunders to renovate a clinical suite originally completed by our team more than 15 years ago.”From Northwestern Endocrinology article
Do you design theaters?+

Yes. Northlight Theatre's new Evanston home includes a 280-seat mainstage, a rehearsal hall, backstage support and a café.

“Located on Church Street, the new cultural destination includes a 280-seat mainstage theatre, rehearsal hall, backstage support spaces, and a café designed to create a welcoming gathering place for audiences and the surrounding community.”From Northlight Theatre article
Do you design high-rise multifamily with affordable units?+

Yes. Julep West Loop is a 25-story tower with 380 apartments, 76 of them affordable.

“The 25-story tower will bring 380 apartments, including 76 affordable residences, to Chicago’s West Loop.”From Julep West Loop article
Do you design youth and education facilities?+

Yes. After School Matters at 1065 N. Orleans won a Chicago Building Congress Merit Award in the Pre K-12 Education category.

“Eckenhoff Saunders is proud to share that the new After School Matters facility at 1065 N. Orleans has received a Chicago Building Congress Merit Award in the Pre K-12 Education category.”From After School Matters award article
Do you account for the neighborhood and code, not just our program?+

Yes. ESA says every design must also address community interests, code and legal requirements, technical specs and engineering.

“In addition to the owner’s program, our work must address the interests of the surrounding community, comply with code and legal requirements, meet demanding technical specifications, consider the building’s orientation and scale, and integrate the art and science of engineering.”From The Firm page (Design Approach)

Not on your site yet

6 questions your site can't answer today.

These answers live in your proposals, fee letters and project files, not on the site.

  • How are your fees structured for a hospital renovation or a ground-up building?Owners budget design fees before they shortlist, and the site never mentions fees.
  • How long does a behavioral health unit renovation take, including IDPH review?Healthcare clients plan bed closures around the schedule, and the site gives no typical durations.
  • How does City of Chicago Self Certification work, and how much permit time does it save?It appears only as a one-line item in the services list, yet it's one of the most valuable things a Chicago owner could hear.
  • How do you keep an occupied hospital running during construction (infection control, phasing, after-hours work)?Project pages mention phased work in departments that stayed open, but none explains how the firm plans infection control, phasing or after-hours work.
  • What do a facility assessment and a feasibility study cost, and what do I get at the end?Both are listed as services with no description, and they're the natural first purchase for a new client.
  • What did comparable projects cost per square foot?Owners use cost benchmarks to test their budget, and the project write-ups give none.
