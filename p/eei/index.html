Not on your site yet

6 questions your site can't answer today.

These answers live in EEI's proposals, project files and inboxes, not on the site.

  • How do you bill for ongoing village engineer services: retainer, hourly, or per project?Village budgets are set a year ahead, so this decides whether EEI fits the line item.
  • How fast do you turn around a developer's plan review for our village?Slow reviews stall development, and staff hear about it from the board and from developers.
  • What does a utility rate study cost, and how long does it take?The Schaumburg story makes buyers ask, and the next step is a budget number.
  • Which communities do you currently serve as municipal engineer?The site says 'several municipalities' without naming them, and buyers want peers to call.
  • Which grants have you won for clients, and how much did they bring in?Grant assistance is promised but no results are shown, and results are what a board compares.
  • Who would be our day-to-day engineer, and how many communities do they cover?Municipal buyers hire the person who will attend their meetings, not the firm in general.
