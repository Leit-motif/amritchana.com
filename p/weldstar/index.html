Not on your site yet

6 questions your site can't answer today.

These answers live in your rate sheets, credit files, delivery routes and your technical team's inboxes, not on the site.

  • What do cylinder rental and demurrage cost?Rent is a recurring line on every gas bill, and while the terms page says cylinders carry a daily rental charge 'at established rates', it never says what those rates are.
  • Is there a delivery fee or order minimum?Small shops weigh delivery against driving to a branch, and no page states what a truck delivery costs or whether a delivery has a minimum order.
  • What's the cutoff to get gas or wire on tomorrow's truck?Running out stops production, and the site gives delivery days but no order cutoff.
  • How much is the restocking fee?The return policy says a fee may be deducted but never gives the amount, so buyers can't price a return.
  • How long does it take to get a robot or cobot cell running?Automation buyers budget around install time, and the site invites them to talk but gives no timeline.
  • How long does credit approval take?The credit account page sends buyers to a PDF application and says nothing about how long approval takes, and a new account can't buy on terms until it clears.
