Not on your site yet

6 questions your site can't answer today.

These answers live in your proposals, board presentations and principals' inboxes, not on the site.

  • How are your fees structured for a district addition or renovation?Business managers need a number for the board packet, and the site says nothing about how fees are set.
  • How long does an addition take from board approval to occupancy, and can construction fit our summer break?School calendars drive every decision, and the site lists 'Project Scheduling' as a service but gives no typical durations.
  • What did comparable projects cost per square foot, final cost against budget?Testimonials praise budget accuracy, but a board wants numbers from projects like its own.
  • How often will someone from DLA be on site during construction?Field representation is listed as a service, but the visit frequency and who comes are not stated.
  • What does your pre-referendum help actually include, and how have those referendums gone?Pre-referendum services are listed in one line; a district weighing a referendum wants scope and results first.
  • Which principal and team would be assigned to our district?Five principals are listed, but nothing says how a district is matched to one or what else that team is carrying.
